SS 304 Steel Letters

Non-magnetic, anti-rust titanium sheet electroplated in gold, rose gold, or black mirror finish.

  • Best For: Jewelry, Luxury Brands

Imported cast acrylic sheets with razor-sharp CNC cutting and bright front LED illumination.

  • Lifespan: 7 - 10 Years

  • Best For: Clinics, Salons, Bakeries

ACP Panel Fascia

Aluminium Composite Panels in matte black, wooden grain, or brushed finish with push-through acrylic.

  • Lifespan: 6 - 8 Years
  • Weatherproof: Rain & Sun Proof
  • Facade: Hides Ugly Wall Surfaces
  • Best For: Full Storefront Remodels
Investment: Moderate & Durable

Backlit Flex Boards

High-resolution solvent or UV-printed backlit star flex stretched over an iron frame with tube/LED lighting.

  • Lifespan: 2 - 4 Years
  • Replacement: Easy & Fast to Re-print
  • Cost: Very Economical
  • Best For: Temporary Promotions, Grocery
Investment: Economy / Entry Level

Our signboards utilize high-efficiency 12V DC injection LEDs (0.72W to 1.2W per module). A standard 10ft × 3ft backlit board running 8 hours every night consumes less than 1.5 units of power per day — costing only around ₹250 to ₹350 per month on your regular commercial meter!
